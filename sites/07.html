Paris

Paris is the capital city of France. It is famous for the Eiffel Tower, museums, and beautiful architecture.

Tokyo

Tokyo is the capital of Japan. It is known for its advanced technology, culture, and vibrant city life.

Switzerland

Switzerland is famous for its mountains, lakes, and beauty. It is a popular destination for tourists.

Canada

Canada is known for its forests, national parks, and scenic landscapes. It offers many outdoor activities.
