Company Profile

Google

Google is a technology company that provides products and services used by people around the world. Its mission is to organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful. Google provides services such as Search, YouTube, Google Maps, and Google Cloud. The company also develops products using artificial intelligence and other technologies. Google focuses on making information and technology easier and more useful for everyone.

About the Company

Google was founded in 1998 and is one of the world's leading technology companies. It develops internet-based products and services for individuals and businesses.
